Each card headlines the cheapest place to buy right now, refreshed daily by a GitHub Action scraper: TCGPlayer (Market price, or the lowest current listing when Market sits below it) or Card Kingdom (in stock only). Decks with no live price show their MSRP for reference only. Singles values come from MTGJSON; "profit to crack" uses the real Card Kingdom buylist and is shown before tax, shipping and fees.
Power scores and brackets from ScryCheck are automated estimates; see ScryCheck's accuracy notes. Scores shown inline where published. Brackets follow Wizards' official system (B1 casual → B5 cEDH).
Data sources: TCGplayer prices via TCGCSV; Card Kingdom prices and stock from Card Kingdom's public pricelist; decklists and card prices from MTGJSON (MIT License); power scores from ScryCheck. Prices are refreshed once a day and can lag the vendors' sites.
